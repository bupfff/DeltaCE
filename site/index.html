The tour

Seven things your calculator couldn't do before.

Δ1

The steps behind the answer

Press enter after an answer and DeltaCE shows how to get it, the way a worked example does: derivatives, integrals, limits, sums and Taylor series, and in algebra, equations and factoring. Every step names its rule.

Δ2

What to do next, one key away

Under each answer, its likeliest next moves: Factor, Solve = 0, Graph, or Derivative and Integral. ▶ picks one, enter does it. No menus to dig through.

Δ3

Graphs that know their key points

Zeros, extrema, inflection points and crossings, found exactly and labeled on the graph. Pan and zoom while a key is held; trace, tables, tangent lines and areas. Conics, parametric and polar curves, slope fields.

Δ4

A function, analyzed

analyze(f) walks through a function the way a test asks: domain, intercepts, symmetry, asymptotes and holes, increasing and decreasing, extrema, concavity, inflection points.

Δ5

Algebra, worked out

Linear and quadratic equations (always by the formula, every value shown), rational and radical equations with each candidate checked, factoring by GCF, special products, grouping or rational roots.

Δ6

Statistics, with the fit in exact form

Type your data in two columns. Each column gets its statistics and a box plot; the points get a fitted line, quadratic, cubic, exponential, power or log curve, exact when it can be, with r and r², a predicted y, and a graph you trace point by point.

Δ7

Units, written properly

A units menu on alpha F4: speeds, energy, pressure and more, Celsius to Fahrenheit, results written the way a textbook writes them.

Install

On your calculator in three steps.

Plug the calculator into a computer, open this page in Chrome or Edge, and click. The page checks the calculator, makes room, sends DeltaCE and starts its installer: press enter once on the calculator and it installs by itself.

  • A TI-84 Plus CE or TI-83 Premium CE on OS 5.3 to 5.8.4, and its USB cable
  • Installing erases the calculator's apps and archived files: back up what you want to keep
  • Safari and Firefox can't reach a calculator: use TI Connect CE there

DeltaCE is a computer algebra system: follow your class and test rules (some tests, like the ACT, don't allow one).

This browser can't reach a calculator. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer, or TI Connect CE.
On Windows: install TI Connect CE 6.1 or newer first. The page uses its USB driver; you don't need to open it.
  1. 1

    Connect

    Plug in the calculator and turn it on. Close TI Connect CE if it's open.

  2. 2

    Install

    Sends DeltaCE.

  3. 3

    Press enter on the calculator

    When it shows INST, press enter once. It installs and restarts by itself ("RAM Cleared"). Then apps → DeltaCE.

Install with TI Connect CE instead
  1. Clear the archive: 2nd + (MEM) → 7 → ▶ ARCHIVE → 3 → 2.
  2. Download DeltaCE.b84 and send it with TI Connect CE.
  3. prgm → enter → enter opens arTIfiCE. enter starts INST, enter again installs.
  4. At "Success! Will now reset", press enter. Then clear, apps → DeltaCE.
Trouble?
  • Connect fails on Windows: update TI Connect CE to 6.1 or newer, then unplug and replug the calculator.
  • Connect fails on a Mac or Chromebook: close TI Connect CE, then unplug and replug.
  • Linux: add a udev rule such as SUBSYSTEM=="usb", ATTRS{idVendor}=="0451", ATTRS{idProduct}=="e008", TAG+="uaccess" in /etc/udev/rules.d/69-ti-calc.rules, then replug.
  • Still not enough memory after the erase: some apps survive it (often TI's language apps). Delete them: 2nd + → 2 → Apps, del on each.
  • "RAM Cleared" but this page still waits: DeltaCE is installed. Press apps → DeltaCE.
  • Frozen at "Success! Will now reset": press the reset button on the back (a pen tip). DeltaCE stays installed.
  • The calculator acts strangely: clear the RAM: 2nd + → 7 → 1 → 2. DeltaCE stays installed.
  • OS 5.8.5 or newer: not supported yet (the installer's arTIfiCE v2.1 works up to 5.8.4).

Something else, or a wrong answer? Open an issue on GitHub with what you typed.

Versions

Nineteen releases, each one a change.

  1. 1.9Statistics: data in two columns, box plots, fitted curves with r², the points on the graph.
  2. 1.8A look of its own: the teal status bar, the answer card, and the action row under each answer.
  3. 1.7Parametric and polar curves, slope fields; engine fixes for the calculator's 24-bit integers.
  4. 1.6Code placed for the flash cache: heavy calculus 6–8% faster.
  5. 1.5Units fixed everywhere and written the way textbooks write them.
  6. 1.4Equations in x and y on the graph: circles, ellipses, hyperbolas.
  7. 1.3The work behind F4's forms: completed square, long division, partial fractions.
  8. 1.264 KB more room.
  9. 1.1Graph tools: value at x, solve f(x) = …, max and min, labels.
  10. 1.0A new graph screen: pan, trace, exact key points, tables.
  11. 0.9Actions on an answer, menus that remember, step navigation.
  12. 0.8Steps for rational and radical equations.
  13. 0.7Factoring steps.
  14. 0.6Steps for linear and quadratic equations.
  15. 0.5Function analysis.
  16. 0.4The teal accent and the Δ mark.
  17. 0.3Celsius and Fahrenheit, the antiderivative check, value at x.
  18. 0.2Physical units: 72 of them in 8 categories.
  19. 0.1The fork: DeltaCE's name and start screen.
