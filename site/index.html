DeltaCE

The calculus of change, shown step by step.

A computer algebra system for your TI-84 Plus CE. Type math the way it looks, get exact answers the way your textbook writes them, and see the steps behind them.

Install What it does

Free and open source. For the TI-84 Plus CE and TI-83 Premium CE. Based on FlowCE and KhiCAS.

What it does

The steps

Press enter after an answer to see how to get it: derivatives, integrals, limits, sums and Taylor series, and in algebra, equations (linear, quadratic, rational, radical) and factoring. Each step names its rule.

Graphs that know their key points

Zeros, extrema, inflection points and crossings, found exactly. Trace, pan and zoom while a key is held, a table, the tangent line, the area. Circles and conics, parametric and polar curves, slope fields.

What to do next, one key away

Under an answer, its likeliest next steps: Factor, Solve = 0, Graph, or Derivative and Integral. ▶ picks one, enter does it. F4 shows the answer's other forms, with their work.

Function analysis

analyze(f) goes through a function the way a test asks: domain, intercepts, symmetry, asymptotes and holes, where it increases and decreases, extrema, concavity, inflection points.

Units

A units menu on alpha F4: lengths, speeds, energy, pressure and more, Celsius to Fahrenheit, written the way a textbook writes them.

Exact answers

π²/6, not 1.64493; ln|sec x + tan x| + C. The decimal value sits right under the exact one. Calculus 1, 2 and 3: series, differential equations, multiple integrals, vectors and matrices too.

Install

You need a TI-84 Plus CE on OS 5.3 to 5.8.4, its USB cable, and Chrome or Edge on a computer (Safari and Firefox can't reach a calculator: use TI Connect CE there). Installing erases the calculator's apps and archived files.

This browser can't reach a calculator. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer, or TI Connect CE.
On Windows: install TI Connect CE 6.1 or newer first. The page uses its USB driver; you don't need to open it.
  1. 1

    Connect

    Plug in the calculator and turn it on. Close TI Connect CE if it's open.

  2. 2

    Install

    Sends DeltaCE.

  3. 3

    Press enter on the calculator

    When it shows INST, press enter once. It installs and restarts by itself ("RAM Cleared"). Then apps → DeltaCE.

Install with TI Connect CE instead
  1. Clear the archive: 2nd + (MEM) → 7 → ▶ ARCHIVE → 3 → 2.
  2. Download DeltaCE.b84 and send it with TI Connect CE.
  3. prgm → enter → enter opens arTIfiCE. enter starts INST, enter again installs.
  4. At "Success! Will now reset", press enter. Then clear, apps → DeltaCE.
Trouble?
  • Connect fails on Windows: update TI Connect CE to 6.1 or newer, then unplug and replug the calculator.
  • Connect fails on a Mac or Chromebook: close TI Connect CE, then unplug and replug.
  • Linux: add a udev rule such as SUBSYSTEM=="usb", ATTRS{idVendor}=="0451", ATTRS{idProduct}=="e008", TAG+="uaccess" in /etc/udev/rules.d/69-ti-calc.rules, then replug.
  • Still not enough memory after the erase: some apps survive it (often TI's language apps). Delete them: 2nd + → 2 → Apps, del on each.
  • "RAM Cleared" but this page still waits: DeltaCE is installed. Press apps → DeltaCE.
  • Frozen at "Success! Will now reset": press the reset button on the back (a pen tip). DeltaCE stays installed.
  • The calculator acts strangely: clear the RAM: 2nd + → 7 → 1 → 2. DeltaCE stays installed.
  • OS 5.8.5 or newer: not supported yet (the installer's arTIfiCE v2.1 works up to 5.8.4).

Something else, or a wrong answer? Open an issue on GitHub with what you typed.
